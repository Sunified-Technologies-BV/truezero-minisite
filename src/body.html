The Problem

Large scale solar is inefficient, opaque, and as an asset, untrusted.

There is a lack of data at panel level that leaves operators, financiers and insurers guessing. This creates the overbuilding of assets and unverified generation data, priced as risk by financiers and insurers.

The net effect
A lower percentage of projects are financeable.
© 2026 Sunified Group BV02 / 07
Our Solution

Sunified’s trust infrastructure starts with an inexpensive deep tech chip.

The Unity Chip is manufactured into every solar panel and battery module, delivering treasury-grade data and energy security.

It measures every 30 seconds and signs each reading with a key that never leaves the silicon. At the gateway, a hardware security module countersigns every payload. Verified readings are sealed into a hash chain and anchored every five minutes.

Hedera Guardian.

“If I can’t digitally define it, it’s going to be really hard to finance, which means it’s not going to live as collateral.”

“A serial number for a carbon credit is not different than an asset that we’ve already dealt with in Web3. If it’s on these digital rails, we’re ready from day one.”

Wes Geisenberger, Hashgraph, at GBBC’s Annual Blockchain Central UNGA. Posted by @hedera, 26 October 2026.11 Trusted data + institution-ready standards + secure custody = carbon credits that can serve as collateral.

Guardian is the open-source policy and dMRV platform our signed readings flow into. The “trusted data” in that equation is what UNITY signs at the panel. On 22 September 2026 a signed test credential minted a certificate on Hedera testnet and a tampered copy was refused.3
