The Problem

Large scale solar is inefficient, opaque, and as an asset, untrusted.

There is a lack of data at panel level that leaves operators, financiers and insurers guessing. This creates the overbuilding of assets and unverified generation data, priced as risk by financiers and insurers.

The net effect
A lower percentage of projects are financeable.
© 2026 Sunified Group BV02 / 07
Our Solution

Sunified’s trust infrastructure starts with an inexpensive deep tech chip.

The Unity Chip is manufactured into every solar panel and battery module, delivering treasury-grade data and energy security.

It measures every 30 seconds and signs each reading with a key that never leaves the silicon. At the gateway, a hardware security module countersigns every payload. Verified readings are sealed into a hash chain and anchored every five minutes.

Hedera · Sustainability Studio

Hedera Guardian

Open-source studio that turns environmental methodologies into policy-as-code, running on the Hedera network.

Guardian digitises, manages and issues environmental assets such as carbon credits, renewable energy certificates and biodiversity credits, with the Hedera network as the public trust layer. Sunified’s UNITY readings are the signed measurement that feeds it: on 22 September 2026 a signed test credential minted a certificate on Hedera testnet and a tampered copy was refused.
